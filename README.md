# Vasudev Studio | The Visual Asset Engine (v3.2)

## The Philosophy
Vasudev Studio isn't just another gradient generator. It is a high-performance visual laboratory built for designers who demand precision and soul in their digital assets. With the v3.2 update, we've introduced an **Ultra-Compact Technical UI** and a surgical JavaScript engine designed for zero-lag professional workflows.

## 🚀 What's New in v3.2

### 📸 Image-to-Palette Extraction
Transform any inspiration into a working palette. Upload any image (JPG, PNG, WEBP) and our local browser engine will automatically extract the most dominant, vibrant colors. No servers, no data leaks—just pure color science.

### 🧪 Advanced Gradient Lab
The Studio now features a comprehensive Gradient Workspace supporting:
*   **Linear & Radial**: Classic flows with multi-point midpoint control.
*   **Conic**: Unique circular sweeps for modern UI elements.
*   **Mesh 2.0**: Multi-layered radial gradients for high-end digital art.
*   **Text Mode**: Apply your gradients directly to typography with a single click.

### 👁️ Contrast Checker (WCAG 2.1)
Professional accessibility is no longer an afterthought. Our built-in checker calculates real-time contrast ratios between any two colors in your palette, providing instant **AA/AAA compliance** feedback for inclusive design.

## The Core Experience

### 🎨 Chromatic Mastery
The sidebar utilizes an **Accordion Architecture** to keep your workspace clean. Tweak individual color points, shuffle palettes for instant inspiration, or lock specific colors to build around a core brand identity.

### 📐 Precision Composition
*   **Multi-Format Canvas**: Instantly switch between **Desktop (16:9)**, **Social (1:1)**, and **Story (9:16)** ratios.
*   **Textural Grain**: Add a layer of analog warmth with our adjustable noise engine, moving your designs from "flat digital" to "premium physical."
*   **Geometric Overlays**: Toggle dot-matrix or architectural grids to add structure to your fluid compositions.

### ⚡ Turbo-Charged Workflow
*   **Surgical DOM Updates**: Our optimized engine updates individual elements without full-page re-renders, ensuring 60fps interaction even on low-power devices.
*   **One-Click Export**: Copy CSS, SCSS, or JSON tokens instantly.
*   **4K PNG Snapshots**: High-fidelity asset generation at 4x scale for professional design hand-off.

## The Architecture
Built with performance and modern standards in mind:
- **Core Engine**: Vanilla JavaScript (Surgical Logic) & Tailwind CSS.
- **Rendering**: Hardware-accelerated CSS Variables and filters.
- **Privacy**: 100% Client-side processing. Your images never leave your machine.
